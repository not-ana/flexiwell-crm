A: No! Use templates + Webflow/WordPress to speed up. Hire freelance designer if needed.

**Q: What if client cancels after free CRM period?**
A: That's why the lifetime discount - incentivizes staying. But you still made $2.8k on the website.

**Q: Is 15-minute onboarding realistic?**
A: Yes! With templates and auto-import, we tested and achieved it. Complex clients may take 30-40min.
