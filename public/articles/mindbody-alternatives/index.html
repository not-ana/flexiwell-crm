10 Best MindBody Alternatives in 2026

Looking for MindBody alternatives? Compare the top 10 gym and studio management software options with better pricing, AI features, and modern interfaces.

FlexiWell Team 5 Jan 2026

Are you looking for MindBody alternatives? You’re not alone. Many studio and gym owners are searching for software that offers better pricing, a more modern interface, and features that MindBody lacks—like AI automation and WhatsApp integration.

In this comprehensive guide, we’ll compare the 10 best MindBody alternatives in 2026, breaking down features, pricing, and the ideal use case for each platform.

Why Studios Are Leaving MindBody

Before exploring alternatives, let’s understand why so many businesses are looking to switch:

  • High costs: MindBody plans can exceed $500/month for full features
  • Complex interface: Steep learning curve and outdated design
  • Transaction fees: High payment processing fees eat into margins
  • No AI features: Missing modern automation capabilities
  • No WhatsApp/Instagram: Can’t meet clients where they are
  • Long contracts: Difficult to cancel or switch providers

2. Momence

Momence has gained popularity as a clean, user-friendly alternative focused on boutique fitness studios.

Pricing: Starting around $99/month

Pros: Easy to use, good mobile app, modern interface

Cons: No AI features, no WhatsApp, can get expensive at scale

Best for: Studios prioritizing simplicity over advanced features

3. WellnessLiving

WellnessLiving positions itself as a direct MindBody competitor with similar features at lower prices.

Pricing: Starting around $89/month

Pros: Free MindBody data migration, comprehensive features

Cons: Interface can be clunky, no AI automation

Best for: Studios wanting MindBody features at a lower cost

4. Glofox

Owned by ABC Fitness Solutions, Glofox offers robust gym management with a focus on branded mobile apps.

Pricing: Contact for quote (starts ~$110/month)

Pros: Branded iOS/Android apps, good for larger gyms

Cons: No transparent pricing, no AI features, enterprise-focused

Best for: Larger gyms wanting custom branded apps

5. Vagaro

Vagaro is a budget-friendly option popular with salons and solo practitioners.

Pricing: Starting at $30/month per user

Pros: Very affordable, includes marketplace

Cons: Per-user pricing adds up, limited advanced features

Best for: Solo instructors on a tight budget

6. Mariana Tek

Mariana Tek (now part of Xplor) focuses on boutique fitness with strong brand customization.

Pricing: Contact for quote (typically $200+/month)

Pros: Beautiful branded experience, enterprise features

Cons: Expensive, complex setup, no AI features

Best for: Premium boutique studios with large budgets

7. Pike13

Pike13 offers straightforward scheduling and client management for fitness businesses.

Pricing: Starting around $118/month

Pros: Clean interface, good scheduling

Cons: Limited marketing features, no AI

Best for: Studios wanting simple, reliable scheduling

8. TeamUp

TeamUp is a UK-based platform popular with CrossFit boxes and group fitness studios.

Pricing: Starting around $99/month

Pros: Good for group classes, member management

Cons: Limited in non-group settings, no AI

Best for: CrossFit boxes and group fitness studios

9. Zen Planner

Zen Planner focuses on martial arts, CrossFit, and fitness studios with solid core features.

Pricing: Starting around $99/month

Pros: Good for martial arts, belt tracking

Cons: Dated interface, no AI features

Best for: Martial arts schools and CrossFit boxes

10. Arketa

Arketa is a newer platform focusing on wellness studios with a modern approach.

Pricing: Starting around $79/month

Pros: Modern interface, good wellness focus

Cons: Smaller company, fewer integrations

Best for: Small wellness studios wanting a fresh platform
