Compare the top Pilates studio software solutions for 2026. Find the best booking, client management, and automation tools for your reformer or mat studio.

FlexiWell Team 5 Jan 2026

Running a successful Pilates studio in 2026 requires more than just great instruction—you need powerful software to manage bookings, clients, and payments. In this comprehensive guide, we’ve analyzed the best Pilates studio software options to help you find the perfect fit for your reformer or mat studio.

What to Look for in Pilates Studio Software

Before diving into our recommendations, here are the key features every Pilates studio should prioritize:

  • Equipment scheduling - Track reformers, chairs, barrels, and other apparatus
  • Class and private session booking - Support for group classes and 1-on-1 sessions
  • Client progress tracking - Monitor goals, injuries, and session notes
  • Package management - Sell class packs, memberships, and private session bundles
  • Automated reminders - Reduce no-shows with email, SMS, and WhatsApp notifications
  • Waitlist management - Handle popular classes and cancellations efficiently
  • Payment processing - Integrated billing with low transaction fees

2. Momence

Momence is a popular choice for boutique fitness studios, including Pilates businesses. It offers a clean interface and solid core features.

Best for: Studios wanting a simple, straightforward booking system

Key Features:

  • Class and appointment booking
  • Membership management
  • Custom branded app
  • Payment processing

Pricing: Starts around $99/month

Pros: Easy to use, good mobile app

Cons: No AI features, no WhatsApp integration, can get expensive

3. MindBody

MindBody is one of the oldest players in the wellness software space. While it offers comprehensive features, many studios find it complex and expensive.

Best for: Large studios wanting maximum marketplace exposure

Key Features:

  • MindBody marketplace for client discovery
  • Comprehensive scheduling
  • Retail and inventory management
  • Staff management

Pricing: Starts around $129/month, can exceed $500+ for full features

Pros: Large marketplace, comprehensive features

Cons: Complex, expensive, outdated interface, high transaction fees

4. WellnessLiving

WellnessLiving positions itself as a more affordable MindBody alternative with similar features.

Best for: Studios migrating from MindBody seeking cost savings

Key Features:

  • Online booking
  • Automated marketing
  • Staff scheduling
  • Client app

Pricing: Starts around $89/month

Pros: Free data migration from MindBody, competitive pricing

Cons: Interface can be clunky, no AI features

5. Vagaro

Vagaro is a budget-friendly option that works well for solo Pilates instructors or very small studios.

Best for: Solo instructors on a tight budget

Key Features:

  • Basic scheduling
  • Payment processing
  • Email marketing
  • Client management

Pricing: Starts at $30/month per user

Pros: Very affordable, includes marketplace

Cons: Limited advanced features, not built for Pilates specifically
