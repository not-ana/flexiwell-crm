Detailed comparison of FlexiWell and Glofox gym management software. Compare pricing, features, AI automation, and more to find the best fit for your fitness business.

When it comes to gym and studio management software, FlexiWell and Glofox are two leading contenders. In this complete comparison guide for 2026, we’ll analyze every aspect to help you choose the right platform for your fitness business.

Overview

FlexiWell is a next-generation studio management platform built with AI at its core. Starting at $49/month, it offers intelligent automation, WhatsApp/Instagram bots, and transparent pricing designed for modern wellness businesses.

Glofox is an established player in the gym management space, owned by ABC Fitness Solutions. It offers a comprehensive suite of tools for gyms and studios, with a focus on member engagement and branded mobile apps.

Who Should Choose FlexiWell?

  • Studios wanting AI automation to handle client queries and bookings 24/7
  • Businesses heavy on WhatsApp/Instagram for client communication
  • Solo instructors or small studios looking for affordable pricing ($49/mo starting)
  • Owners who value transparent pricing without sales calls
  • Tech-forward studios wanting API access and modern features

Who Should Choose Glofox?

  • Larger gyms prioritizing branded native mobile apps
  • Studios needing extensive integrations with other ABC Fitness products
  • Businesses preferring enterprise sales relationships with custom pricing
  • Gyms focused on member engagement through app-based features

The Bottom Line

FlexiWell is the better choice for studios that want to leverage AI and automation to save time and improve client experience. The WhatsApp and Instagram bots, AI support assistant, and transparent pricing make it ideal for modern wellness businesses.

Glofox remains a solid choice for larger gyms that prioritize branded mobile apps and are comfortable with enterprise-style sales processes.
